# Research Basis and Evidence Boundaries

**Status:** living evidence register
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-21

## Purpose

This document records which public sources influenced SymType, what design direction each source can reasonably support, and what it cannot establish. It is not a systematic review and does not convert correlations, studies in other motor tasks, or framework papers into causal product claims.

The practice mix, weakness formula, accuracy gates, micro-block length, retention schedule, and adaptive policy are **testable engineering hypotheses**. They must remain versioned, configurable, observable, and compared against a simpler local baseline. SymType must say “based on your observed typing data,” not “scientifically diagnosed.”

## Engineering hypotheses and falsification plan

| Hypothesis                                                                              | Operational comparison                                                                                | Primary evidence                                                                                                            | Stop/modify condition                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feature-aware scheduling improves efficiency over a single-character weakness baseline. | Seeded adaptive scheduler versus deterministic local “keybr-like baseline,” balanced by date/session. | Correct characters or active minutes to a stable WPM/accuracy threshold; 24h/72h and 7-day retention; unseen-text transfer. | No conclusion below the preregistered local sample threshold; simplify or retune if credible effect is absent or adverse. |
| Blocked → interleaved → natural transfer is better than focus-only repetition.          | Repeated feature blocks with and without staged interleaving/transfer.                                | Delayed retention, unseen transfer gap, error recovery, effective correct characters/minute.                                | Reduce interleaving when accuracy, fatigue, or exit behavior worsens without later benefit.                               |
| Sequence targeting adds value when characters are individually adequate.                | Bigram/trigram-focused blocks versus matched character-focused blocks.                                | Sequence IKI/error change with component-key performance controlled, plus natural-context transfer.                         | Do not target unsupported n-grams; retire the rule if improvement does not transfer or causes overuse.                    |
| Conservative mastery estimates reduce false promotion.                                  | Posterior/robust stable-speed gate versus peak/recent mean.                                           | Calibration of predicted error/difficulty, Brier score/log loss, regression after 24h/72h.                                  | Recalibrate priors/intervals if predictions are systematically over- or under-confident.                                  |
| Short, spaced retests improve durable performance.                                      | Time-balanced schedules differing in spacing.                                                         | Retention and total active minutes/characters, not same-session peak.                                                       | Do not infer causality from an uncontrolled personal time series; show uncertainty and confounds.                         |

All experiment variants store algorithm version, assignment rule, exposure, exclusions, and sample count. The UI never displays “statistically significant” unless a documented analysis supports that statement; the default sparse state is “not enough evidence yet.”

## Clean-room and license boundary

1. Keybr is used only as a public functional reference: public README claims and user-supplied screenshots may be inventoried as behavior, never copied as expression.
2. No Keybr source file, test, stylesheet, data file, icon, sound, copy, generated bundle, or screen geometry is imported, translated, traced, or used as a coding template.
3. Engineers implement from this specification, the explicit Symmetric mapping table, original designs/content, browser standards, and independently selected dependencies.
4. SymType does not depend on, link to, or redistribute the Keybr codebase. Any future use of AGPL material requires an explicit legal/licensing decision and a separate decision record; it is outside the current scope.
5. The “keybr-like baseline” is deliberately narrow and named as an approximation: deterministic single-character weakness weighting for local research comparison only.
6. Screenshots are evaluated for a feature inventory and mapping cross-check, not pixel matching. The initial workspace contains no screenshots, so the cross-check remains blocked until they are supplied.
7. Bundled prose, game narrative, code samples, pseudowords, tones, icons, and visual components are original or have documented compatible provenance. `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` records all redistributed material and licenses.

## Claim language

Preferred:

- “Keys assigned to the left index zone were slower in this sample.”
- “The scheduler prioritized this bigram because its median valid IKI was slower than your goal and it is due for a retest.”
- “This session improved practice performance; retention has not yet been measured.”
- “The adaptive strategy used fewer correct characters in this local comparison; the sample is too small for a conclusion.”

Prohibited:

- “We detected your left index finger.”
- “You have damaged muscle memory.”
- “Science proves 40% focus practice is optimal.”
- “SymType guarantees faster learning than Keybr.”
- “This one peak means the key is mastered.”

## Review cadence

Recheck URLs, license declarations, and vendor guidance before a release candidate. New research changes a product default only through a decision record, tests/simulation, and an update to this evidence register; citations alone do not authorize a claim.
